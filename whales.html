Whale watch

Reads the twenty largest holders of a token straight from the chain every thirty seconds and compares each balance to the last reading. A balance that falls is a wallet selling — you see it as it happens, not after the candle.

Movement log

This reads token account balances, so it catches selling and transfers out — it cannot tell you what a wallet paid or what it is worth to them. Balances also move for harmless reasons: moving to a new wallet, bridging, depositing to an exchange. Treat a falling balance as a reason to look, and remember that the largest holder is normally the liquidity pool itself, which moves constantly. Nothing here is financial advice.
